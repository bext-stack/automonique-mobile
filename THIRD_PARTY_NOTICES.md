# Third-party notices

Automonique Mobile depends on Expo, React, React Native, Expo Router, TanStack
Query, Async Storage, and their transitive dependencies. Each dependency
retains the license declared in its distributed package. The exact resolved
versions are recorded in `package-lock.json`.

The canonical `@automonique/sdk` package is vendored from the pinned Automonique
source commit recorded in `vendor/automonique-sdk.json`. It is licensed under
Apache-2.0 and carries its license inside the package archive.

The initial icon and splash assets were generated by the Expo application
template and remain subject to Expo's applicable notices. Before a store or
binary distribution, generate a complete dependency inventory and include all
license texts and notices required by the resolved packages.
